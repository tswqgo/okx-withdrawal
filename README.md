## The network is the most important detail

Crypto addresses can look similar while belonging to different networks. For example, a stablecoin may be available through several chains, such as Ethereum, Tron, BNB Chain, or another supported network.

The rule is simple:

> The asset and network selected on OKX must match the asset and network supported by the receiving wallet or exchange.

Suppose the receiving exchange gives you a USDT deposit address for TRC20. You need to select USDT and TRON on OKX. Sending through ERC20 or another network may result in delayed recovery or permanent loss, depending on the receiving platform.

Do not choose a network only because its displayed fee looks lower. First confirm that the destination supports it. A cheaper transfer sent through the wrong chain is not a bargain; it is an expensive lesson.

### Destination tags and memos

Some assets require more than a wallet address. XRP, for example, may require a destination tag. Other networks and assets may use a memo or similar identifier.

If the receiving platform provides both an address and a tag, memo, or comment, enter both. OKX specifically warns that omitting a required tag can cause the transfer to be lost or require a recovery process.

### A safer first-transfer process

For a new wallet or exchange, use this sequence:

1. Copy the address from the receiving platform.
2. Confirm the asset name.
3. Confirm the network.
4. Check whether a tag or memo is required.
5. Send a small test amount if the transfer is significant.
6. Wait for the test deposit to arrive.
7. Send the remaining balance.

This adds one transaction and possibly another fee, but it is usually cheaper than trying to recover funds sent to the wrong destination.

## How much does an OKX withdrawal cost?

There is no single universal OKX withdrawal fee. The charge depends on the asset, network, region, and withdrawal method.

For crypto withdrawals, OKX states that the fee is shown in the withdrawal screen and can vary between networks. Deposits do not incur a network fee on the OKX side, but the party sending crypto out of OKX pays the withdrawal charge.

For cash withdrawals, OKX displays the applicable processing fee before submission. Depending on the method, the charge may be:

- A flat fee
- A percentage fee
- A combination of a percentage and a flat fee
- No fee for a particular method

The fee is deducted from the withdrawal amount. For example, if you request a cash withdrawal of $100 and the displayed fee is $2, the account may be debited by $100 while the bank receives $98.

### Why the fee changes

A crypto withdrawal fee can change because:

- The asset uses a different blockchain
- The network is congested
- OKX updates its fee group or withdrawal schedule
- The asset has different minimum and maximum limits
- The selected route or destination method is different

Do not rely on an old blog post that lists a fixed BTC, ETH, or USDT withdrawal fee. The number shown in your own withdrawal preview is the relevant figure for that transaction.

## Internal transfers: the cheaper option for another OKX user

If the recipient also uses OKX, an internal transfer may be more convenient than an on-chain withdrawal.

OKX supports internal transfers using recipient details such as:

- Phone number
- Email address
- UID
- Sub-account information

The recipient needs to be a verified user. OKX describes internal transfers as instant and fee-free, although normal account restrictions and verification checks may still apply. A submitted internal transfer can also be cancelled within one minute, according to the help documentation.

Use an internal transfer when:

- You are sending funds to a trusted OKX user
- The recipient does not need the assets on an external blockchain address
- You want to avoid an on-chain network fee
- You need an internal movement rather than a blockchain transaction

Do not use it when the recipient needs a deposit on another exchange or in a self-custody wallet. In that case, an on-chain withdrawal is required.

## Why is my OKX withdrawal pending or blocked?

A pending or unavailable withdrawal does not always mean that the funds are lost. Common causes include:

### Recent security changes

OKX may impose a 24-hour withdrawal restriction after changes to:

- Login password
- Phone number
- Email address
- Authenticator settings
- Other security credentials

The restriction is designed to prevent someone who has taken over an account from immediately moving the funds elsewhere. It normally lifts automatically after the stated period.

### Reaching the withdrawal limit

If the account has used its available 24-hour withdrawal allowance, the withdrawal page should show that the limit has been reached. The remaining amount may be visible on the withdrawal screen, especially on the web interface.

### P2P or risk-control restrictions

A recent P2P purchase can trigger a temporary T+N restriction. OKX may also restrict funds associated with deposit confirmation or double-spending risk controls.

These restrictions are not normally fixed by repeatedly pressing Withdraw. Check the exact message shown in the account and wait for the stated period or complete any requested verification.

### Funds are being used elsewhere

Assets may be unavailable for withdrawal because they are:

- Locked as trading margin
- In Simple Earn
- In On-chain Earn
- Held in a sub-account
- Still waiting for the required blockchain confirmations

Move the funds back to the main account, close or reduce positions, redeem the Earn position, or wait for confirmations as applicable.

### Maintenance or a suspended asset

OKX can temporarily suspend withdrawals during maintenance or upgrades. A specific token may also become unavailable if it is delisted or no longer supported.

Check the asset status and OKX service status before assuming the problem is related to your address or account.

### High-risk address review

OKX may temporarily restrict withdrawals if its risk controls flag the destination address or activity. Its help center states that a high-risk address review can result in a temporary 48-hour restriction. Check the registered email address for instructions and avoid responding to unofficial accounts that ask for funds, QR scans, or verification codes.

## What to do when the recipient has not received the funds

Start with the transaction status inside OKX.

### If the withdrawal is still processing

The transaction may not yet have been broadcast or completed through OKX’s internal checks. Give the status time to update, especially during maintenance or heavy network traffic.

### If there is a transaction ID

Open the transaction on the relevant blockchain explorer and compare:

- Sending asset
- Receiving address
- Network
- Amount
- Confirmation count

If the blockchain shows successful delivery but the receiving exchange has not credited the deposit, contact the receiving exchange first. Its deposit policy may require additional confirmations, a memo, a tag, or manual review.

### If the address or network was wrong

Contact the receiving platform and OKX support as soon as possible. Recovery is not guaranteed. Blockchain transfers generally cannot be reversed by clicking a Cancel button after they have been confirmed.

Before every large OKX withdrawal, confirm these items:

- The account has completed the required verification
- The destination address belongs to the intended recipient
- The asset is correct
